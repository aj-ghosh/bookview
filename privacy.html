Privacy Policy — BookView

Privacy Policy

BookView LLC · Last updated November 23, 2026

The short version: BookView is built so we never need your clients' personal information — and we don't want it. We collect your account email, the case-status board you build, and what's needed to run VA and billing. We don't sell personal information, and we don't run advertising trackers.

What we collect

  • Account information: your name and email address, and sign-in identifiers if you use a social sign-in option.
  • Your board: the case labels, statuses, dates, task notes, license and appointment dates, and commission figures you enter, import, or confirm through VA. Board entries use labels you choose — they are not required to identify any client.
  • VA conversations: your messages to VA, VA's drafts, and the workflow preferences VA remembers for you (for example, how you like stuck cases flagged). You can view and delete VA's memory.
  • Usage and device data: basic logs needed to run and secure the service (pages used, timestamps, error reports).
  • Founding list: if you join the founding list, we store the email you submit, via our form provider, to send launch invitations.

What we deliberately do not collect

BookView is designed to operate without client personal information. Our import tools strip identifier fields before storage, and VA refuses or redacts identifiers in messages. If client personal information is submitted despite this design, we delete or redact it when detected and do not use it for any purpose.

Payments

Payments are processed by our payment processor (Stripe). We never see or store your full card number. We keep billing records such as your plan, credit balance, and transaction history.

How VA processes your requests

VA requests are processed by an AI model provider acting as our service provider. Before a request is sent, identifiers are stripped; requests contain your board's labels and status data, not client identities. VA conversation data is not used to train models for other customers.

How we use information

  • To provide and operate BookView, including your board, VA, and credit balance.
  • To send service emails (invitations, receipts, credit and account notices, changes to these policies).
  • To secure the service and prevent misuse.

We do not sell personal information, share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, or use it to market third-party products.

Cookies

We use only the cookies and local storage needed to keep you signed in and remember your board and preferences. We do not run third-party advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

Retention and deletion

Your board and account data are kept while your account is active. If you cancel, you can export your board; account data is deleted within 30 days of a deletion request, except billing records we must keep for tax and accounting. Founding-list emails are kept until launch invitations are complete or you ask us to remove yours.

Your California privacy rights

Under the CCPA/CPRA, California residents may request to know, delete, or correct the personal information we hold about them, and to opt out of any "sale" or "sharing" — we do neither. To exercise a right, email goajaxo@gmail.com. We will not discriminate against you for exercising a right.

Security

We use encryption in transit, access controls, and identifier-stripping by design. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affecting your information occurs, we will notify you as required by law.

Changes and contact

Material changes to this policy will be announced by email before they take effect. Questions or requests: BookView LLC, goajaxo@gmail.com.
