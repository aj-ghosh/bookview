Why agents lose money quietly

A stuck case isn't an admin problem — it's a paycheck sitting in someone else's queue. Most agents find out a case stalled when the client calls, weeks later. BookView exists so you see it the day it happens.

⏱️

What's stuck, aged

Every pending case carries its age. Anything sitting more than 7 days flags itself — oldest first, with the commission attached to it, so you chase the money in the right order.

🧭

Every line, every channel

Channel dashboards only show the business you placed through them. BookView is channel-neutral: your whole book — every upline, every direct appointment, plus the advisory side — in one view.

💵

What you're owed

Commissions pending, split clean vs. stuck. Renewals coming in the next 30 days before they lapse. Chargeback risk where you can still save it. The money view, not the data-entry view.
